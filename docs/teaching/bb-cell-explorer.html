What this computes. Ranks, k, the relation space K = Ann(A) ∩ Ann(B), the seam-carry map Δ and the Bézout certificate for (R) are all exact 𝔽₂ linear algebra done in your browser. Distance is not computed — it is exponentially hard, and the whole point of the doubling theorem is to avoid searching for it. Published distances shown on presets are quoted, not derived.

Conventions match experiments/bb_lab: HX = (MA | MB), HZ = (MBᵀ | MAᵀ), so the Z-check at g is (Bδg, Aδg) and the X-check at g is (ι(A)δg, ι(B)δg) with ι the inversion g ↦ −g. Section references point at docs/teaching/bb-doubling-theorem.tex.
